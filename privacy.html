Privacy Policy

Rehab Colorado is an independent information and referral website. It has no contact forms, accounts, or site-added advertising analytics tracker.

Website visits

Hosting providers and external font, icon, and component services may receive technical request information, including IP address, browser type, and requested files.

Phone inquiries and health information

Calls may connect to an independent provider or referral partner, not a clinician. The answering party may receive your telephone number and information you choose to share. Before disclosing health or insurance details, ask who is answering, which organization receives the information, how it will be used, whether the call is recorded, and what privacy protections apply. We do not promise that calls are confidential, anonymous, or covered by a particular healthcare privacy rule.

Your choices

You may choose not to call or share information. Direct questions about records or deletion requests to the organization that received your information. Public resources linked from this website have their own privacy practices.
